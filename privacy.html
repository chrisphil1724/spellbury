Spellbury

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Spellbury does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything your family adds stays on your iPad.

Who we are

Spellbury is a spelling practice app for children, made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the maker of Spellbury, and "you" means the parent, guardian or teacher using the app with a child.

Information we collect

None. Spellbury has no sign-up, no user accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect names, email addresses, ages, locations, device identifiers, usage statistics or any other information about you or your child.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or data-collection tools.

What stays on your iPad

To work, the app saves a few things in its own storage on your device. We never receive a copy.

WhatWhy
Word lists and example sentencesSo your child can practise them
Practice results and progress statsTo show which words are mastered and which need work
Voice recordings a grown-up makes of words and sentencesPlayed back during practice instead of the built-in voice
Settings (voice, speed, sounds)To remember your choices

Deleting the app deletes all of this. If you use an iPad backup (iCloud or a computer), Apple may include the app's data in that backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when a grown-up taps Record to record a word or sentence. Recordings are saved on the iPad and are never uploaded or shared. You can delete any recording in the app, and you can turn off microphone access at any time in the iPad's Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan a printed spelling list, the app reads the words from the photo using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is not saved by the app and is never sent anywhere. Only the words you choose to add are kept.

Speech and sentences

Words are read aloud with Apple's built-in voices on the iPad. Example sentences come from a list built into the app or are written by Apple's on-device language model, where available. All of this happens on the device; your words are not sent to us or to any other service.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the full version has been bought, so it can unlock it.

Children's privacy

Spellbury is designed for children and follows Apple's rules for the Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. Purchases, the settings area and any link that leaves the app are behind a grown-ups-only check.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. We will not start collecting personal information from children.

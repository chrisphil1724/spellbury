Common questions

How do I restore the full version on a new iPad?

Open Spellbury, tap the gear on My Words, answer the grown-up question, and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. With Family Sharing on, family members can restore it too.

I bought the full version but it's still locked.

Tap Restore purchase as above. If your family uses Ask to Buy, the purchase unlocks once the family organiser approves it. Still stuck? Email us.

The voice sounds robotic. Can it sound better?

Yes. Download one of Apple's free Premium voices: on the iPad open Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak (Spoken Content on older iPads) > Voices > English, and download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced. Then pick it in Spellbury's settings. In the full version you can also record your own voice saying each word and sentence.

How do I record my own voice?

In the full version, tap Record your voice on My Words. Spellbury goes through your list one word at a time: record the word, then its sentence. You can also tap any word to record or redo just that one. If Record doesn't work, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone on the iPad.

Can I scan my child's spelling list?

Yes, in the full version. Tap Paste or scan a list, then take a photo or choose one. Check the words it found, then add them.

Is my child's information safe?

Spellbury collects nothing. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and word lists, progress and recordings stay on your iPad. Read the privacy policy.

How do I delete our data?

You can delete words, lists and recordings inside the app, and reset stats from the Stats screen. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.
